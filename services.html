FAQ

Questions we hear all the time.

How long does a website take?

Most brochure sites launch in 2–3 weeks from kickoff. eCommerce builds typically take 4–6 weeks depending on catalogue size.

Do I need to provide content?

We can write it for you — copywriting is included in the Growth package. If you have photos and notes, great; if not, we'll source stock imagery.

What if I need changes after launch?

Every project includes 30 days of free tweaks. After that, small edits are covered by a care plan or billed at a flat £60/hour.

Do you work with businesses outside the UK?

Absolutely. We're remote-first and work with clients across Europe, the US, and Australia.

Who owns the website?

You do — 100%. Code, design, content, and domain are all yours, with full handover on final payment.
