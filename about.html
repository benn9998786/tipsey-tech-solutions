Home/About

A small studio with big-agency output.

We're designers, developers, and word-people who got tired of seeing local businesses stuck with slow, forgettable websites.

Our story

Founded in 2019 on one simple idea.

Most small businesses don't need a 60-page strategy deck — they need a website that loads fast, explains what they do, and makes it easy to get in touch. We started Tipsey Tech Solutions to deliver exactly that, without the agency bloat or hourly-meter anxiety.

Today we're a remote-first team of six, working with clients across India. We still take on only a handful of new projects a month, because attention is the one thing that doesn't scale.

What we believe

Four rules we don't bend.

Deadlines are promises

The date we give you is the date we hit. If we ever see a risk, you hear about it early — not on the day.

Plain English only

No jargon, no mystery invoices. If you can't explain it to a friend, we haven't done our job properly.

Quality over volume

We turn down more projects than we take. Fewer clients means each site gets the attention it deserves.

You own everything

Domain, code, content, accounts — all in your name from day one. Never any lock-in or hostage situations.

Results over awards

We'd rather double your enquiries than win a design trophy. Pretty is the baseline; performing is the goal.

After launch matters

A website is a living thing. We stick around with care plans, updates, and honest advice as you grow.

The journey

How we got here.

  1. 2019

    Two people, one spare room

    Founded by a designer and a developer who'd built sites together for years and wanted to do it their own way.

  2. 2021

    The care plan model

    We launched monthly care plans so clients stopped choosing between a new site and a site that stays healthy.

  3. 2023

    eCommerce division

    Added full store builds and migrations, taking on our first 1,000-product catalogue project.

  4. 2025

    50th project, remote-first

    Went fully distributed across four timezones without missing a single launch date.

  5. Today

    120+ sites and counting

    A team of six, still taking a handful of new clients a month — and still answering the phone ourselves.
